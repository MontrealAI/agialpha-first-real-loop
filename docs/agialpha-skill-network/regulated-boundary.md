# Regulated Boundary

Generated jobs, skills, and imports that touch regulated financial, legal, medical, HR, credit, insurance, biometric, law-enforcement, migration, education-access, critical-infrastructure-control, energy-market, utility-market, or offensive-cyber decisioning are blocked or quarantined.

The engine emits boundary statuses such as `regulated_boundary_blocked` or `documentation_only_human_review_required` and does not activate regulated-domain skills.
